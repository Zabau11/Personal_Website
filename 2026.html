2026 So Far

April 19, 2026

2026 started fast. I built Clearize, my first major product, with a mix of logic and "vibe coding." The workflow changed how I think about building, and the project is now close to launch. More than anything, it confirmed that I want to make AI tools approachable for people who are still finding their way into the technology.

On the personal side, I completed my first official race: a 10K in Cluj-Napoca. Training taught me the discipline and grit required to set a difficult goal and see it through.

I also joined my first hackathon at Polihack. My team built a RAG tool for lawyers, took third place, and I handled the frontend and UI. I left with new skills, great connections, and even more motivation to keep improving.

Recently, I started doing growth at UGC Agent, an app made by my friend Vic. I'm grateful he gave me the chance to prove myself, and that's what I'm focused on right now: cold outreach, starting email campaigns, and shipping a couple of features. I have to say, I love it.

It's been an action-packed year so far, and I'm ready for whatever comes next.
